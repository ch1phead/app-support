Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Restaurant Wishlist does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and has no backend service.

Data stored on your device

Recent restaurant and cuisine suggestions are stored locally on your device so you can reuse them. You can remove this data by deleting the app.

Messages

When you share a wishlist or vote update, the content is sent through Apple Messages to the conversation you choose. Restaurant Wishlist does not receive or store a copy of that conversation data. Apple’s privacy policies apply to Messages and iCloud.

Analytics and tracking

Restaurant Wishlist does not use advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or data brokers.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support page.
