Golden Desk

Support

Golden Desk helps you find nearby places to work, check today’s closing time, and build a schedule around the time you have left.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Nearby places look wrong: In Settings, open Privacy & Security → Location Services, select Golden Desk, and allow location access with Precise Location enabled. Then reopen Discover and search again.
  • Hours are missing or look outdated: Open the place in Apple Maps, confirm today’s hours, and use Golden Desk’s refresh-hours action. Apple Maps business hours can change and are not guaranteed to be available for every place.
  • Notifications are not appearing: In Settings, open Notifications → Golden Desk and allow notifications. Then check the app’s notification settings and confirm the stop still has a closing time and reminder enabled.
  • A widget or Live Activity is stale: Open Golden Desk once, then remove and add the widget again. iOS controls refresh timing, so updates may not be immediate.
  • The app is behaving unexpectedly: Quit and reopen Golden Desk. If the issue continues, restart the device and check for an updated version.

Report a bug or request help

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your device model, iOS version, Golden Desk version, what you expected, and what happened. GitHub issues are public, so please do not include sensitive personal information or a precise home location.
