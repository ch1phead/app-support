Time Progress

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Time Progress is designed to work privately on your device.

Data collection

Time Progress does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage information. No account is required.

Tracking and advertising

Time Progress does not track users, display advertising, or use advertising identifiers. The app does not include analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking services.

On-device calculations

Time calculations are performed locally on your device using its system date, time, calendar, and time-zone settings. Core app functionality does not require an internet connection.

Settings, widgets, and reminders

Your display preferences and reminder choices are stored locally on your device. A private Apple App Group shares relevant preferences between Time Progress and its widgets. If you enable milestone reminders, they are scheduled and delivered locally by the operating system. This information is not sent to the developer.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request on GitHub. GitHub issues are public, so please do not include sensitive personal information.

Changes to this policy

If Time Progress’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.
