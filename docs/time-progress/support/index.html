Time Progress

Support

Time Progress visualizes how far you are through the day, week, month, and year using your device’s system date and time.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Progress looks incorrect: Confirm that Date & Time, time zone, and calendar settings are correct in the Settings app, then reopen Time Progress.
  • A widget is not updating: Open Time Progress once, then remove and add the widget again. iOS controls widget refresh timing, so updates may not be immediate.
  • Reminders are not appearing: In Settings, open Notifications, select Time Progress, and confirm notifications are allowed. Then verify the desired milestone is enabled in the app.
  • The app is behaving unexpectedly: Quit and reopen the app. If the issue continues, restart the device and check for an updated version.

Report a bug or request help

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your device model, iOS version, Time Progress version, what you expected, and what happened. GitHub issues are public, so please do not include sensitive personal information.
